# Keep the lesson and its closure evidence

Use the [incident template](../project/incidents.md) to record symptom, first failed operation, actual state, cause confidence, repair and prevention. Keep unknowns explicit and link evidence rather than relying on a narrative alone.

Choose a prevention action that another maintainer can check. It may be a clearer operation, an enforced invariant, an automated test, a corrected default or a revised procedure. Assign its owner and completion condition.

Verify the original action after repair. Check fresh-install instructions and affected examples so the next operator does not recreate the defect. Record source checks, runtime rehearsal and user acceptance separately.

Retain failed attempts and partial-state evidence that matter for future recovery. Keep sensitive payloads and credentials in approved storage. Publish only the reusable lesson and evidence appropriate for the audience.

Do not erase earlier uncertainty after learning the cause. Preserve the reason an anchor or conclusion was superseded. A later scope change is a new requirement, not evidence that the original acceptance promise was different.
